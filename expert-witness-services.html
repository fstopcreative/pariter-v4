ABOUT OUR SERVICE

Evidence That Assists the Court.

When a dispute depends on technical evidence, the quality of the expert report can shape the outcome. Pariter's expert witnesses provide clear, well-reasoned opinions on building, land and valuation matters, prepared in line with RICS professional standards and the duties owed to the court or tribunal.

Our role is to assist the court, not to advocate for either party. We review the evidence, inspect the property, and set out our findings in plain English so that judges, tribunal members, solicitors and clients can understand the technical issues clearly.

OUR PROCESS

Initial review

We review the instructions and documents and confirm we have no conflict of interest.

FAQS

Frequently Asked Questions.

What does an expert witness do? expand_more

An expert witness gives an independent technical opinion to help a court or tribunal understand issues outside its own knowledge.

Who can instruct an expert witness? expand_more

Instructions usually come from solicitors, but we also accept instructions from individuals and organisations, including as a single joint expert appointed by both parties.

Can you help before proceedings start? expand_more

Yes. We can provide early advisory reports to help clients understand the strength of their position before deciding whether to proceed.

How long does a report take? expand_more

Timescales depend on the complexity of the matter and any court deadlines. We will confirm a realistic timetable when we receive your instructions.
