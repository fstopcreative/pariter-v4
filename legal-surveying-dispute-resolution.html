Legal Surveying & Dispute Resolution

Surveyor Advocacy Services

During initial negotiations and when disputes reach a tribunal, court, public inquiry or formal hearing, effective representation can be critical to achieving the best possible outcome. Pariter's Surveyor Advocacy service provides specialist property and construction-focused representation, combining technical surveying expertise with professional advocacy skills to present and advance your case clearly, professionally and persuasively.

Our surveyor advocates act on behalf of property owners, developers, landlords, tenants, contractors and professional clients in a wide range of property, construction and land-related disputes. We prepare cases, review evidence, draft submissions, challenge opposing evidence where appropriate, and represent clients throughout proceedings while maintaining the highest professional and ethical standards.

By combining technical knowledge of buildings, land, valuation and construction with dispute resolution expertise, Pariter can often provide a practical and cost-effective alternative to traditional legal representation in suitable matters. Where a matter would be better served by specialist legal counsel, we will advise clients accordingly and work collaboratively alongside solicitors and barristers as part of the wider professional team.
